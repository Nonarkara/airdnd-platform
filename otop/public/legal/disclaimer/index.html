1. No warranty of accuracy ไม่รับประกันความถูกต้อง

Every number, name, address, phone number, star grade, and category on this site comes from a CSV published by a state agency officer, often years ago. We re-publish it. We do not warrant that it is accurate, current, or complete. We do not warrant that a phone number rings the right person. We do not warrant that a producer is still producing. We do not warrant that a star grade means what you think it means in 2026.

The Thai star-grade system, as published by the Department of Community Development, defines 5★ as a product with a score of 90–100 in the most recent OTOP Product Champion selection; the selection is held every two years, and a 5★ from 2018 is not the same as a 5★ from 2024. The site carries the year of selection where the source carries it.

We use them descriptively, which is the only right anyone has to use them.

A listing on this site is not a recommendation of the producer. A "Reachable Champion" label is not a quality award from us; it is a description of a record on data.go.th that carries both a 5★ grade and a phone number.

6. No liability for derived fields ไม่รับผิดต่อข้อมูลที่วิเคราะห์ได้

The site's derived fields (contact tier, completeness gate, findings, hero matchmaker scores, province dossier rankings) are computed from the source. They are descriptive, not evaluative. A "no contact channel" tier means the published record carries no phone number; it does not mean the producer has no phone. A "quality leader" province is a province with a high proportion of 4-5★ records in the source; it is not a statement about the actual quality of the producers' products.

8. Specific warnings คำเตือนเฉพาะ

  • Phone numbers may be wrong. The data is years old in places. Verify by SMS, not by surprise voice call. If a producer asks you to stop, stop.
  • Addresses may be imprecise. The latitude and longitude come from the tambon centroid in the source, not from the producer's actual door. A 5 km error is not unusual.
  • Star grades are not safety permits. A 5★ food product is not the same as a product that has passed อย. registration. Check both before you consume, especially if you have allergies.
  • Cross-reference numbers (population, tourism, internet) come from third parties. They are not from the CDD and are not warrantied by us. See the citation on each view.
  • Forwarded calls, advance fees, and personal meetings are out of scope. If a producer asks for a deposit, a meeting at a remote location, or a transfer to a personal account, the producer is responsible for that decision. We do not mediate disputes.
