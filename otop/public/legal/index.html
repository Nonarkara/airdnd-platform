How this site handles its data — and yours

เว็บไซต์นี้จัดการข้อมูลอย่างไร — ของคุณและของเรา

This platform is operated by Dr Non Arkaraprasertkul, a senior expert at Thailand's depa. It is built on public data published by the Department of Community Development on data.go.th. The pages below explain what that means in practice: who can use the site, what data is republished, where it came from, and how to ask for a correction.

In one paragraph สรุปสั้น ๆ

The data you see is the Thai government's own open data, republished. We do not collect new personal data from visitors; we do not run tracking; we do not sell the data. We add a few derived fields — a contact tier, a freshness stamp, a finding — and we are clear about which is source and which is ours. The five pages above say so in the formal language a lawyer would use; this page is the short version. The full text governs.
