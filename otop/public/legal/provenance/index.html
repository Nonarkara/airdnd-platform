Data provenance & license

แหล่งที่มาของข้อมูลและสัญญาอนุญาต — ข้อมูลมาจากไหน อนุญาตให้ใช้อย่างไร เราเพิ่มอะไรบ้าง

Every record on this site traces back to a single point: the open OTOP catalogue published by the Department of Community Development on Thailand's central open-data portal, data.go.th. This page names the source, the licence, the attribution, and the things we add on top.

Official portal (separate surface)otop.cdd.go.th — the Odoo-backed CDD operator portal, the most authoritative non-open-data view

The site pulls the open CSV catalogue directly. We do not scrape the otop.cdd.go.th portal, and we do not use the token-gated otopapi.cdd.go.th:3000 endpoint; both are out of bounds for us. Everything you see on this site traces back to a CSV on data.go.th and its provincial mirrors.

2. The licences we re-publish under สัญญาอนุญาตที่ใช้เผยแพร่

The data.go.th portal operates under the EGA Open Government License, which is compatible with the family of open licences listed below. Individual datasets in the OTOP catalogue carry per-resource licence tags; the most common in our pull are:

G2CC BY-NonCommercial 4.0Copy, modify, redistribute, non-commercial onlyYesNo

The per-resource licence tag is preserved in our raw cache and is the licence under which each record is re-published here. Where the source tag is unclear, we default to the most permissive G0 / Open Data Common reading.

3. What the licence does not cover สิ่งที่สัญญาอนุญาตไม่ครอบคลุม

The OGD licence and the CC family permit the use of the data. They do not cover the personal data contained within the data. The NSTDA presentation on Thailand's open-data programme (2018) is explicit on this point: "การอนุญาตให้ใช้ข้อมูลนี้ ไม่ครอบคลุมถึง 'ข้อมูลส่วนบุคคลที่อยู่ในเนื้อหาข้อมูล'" — the licence to use the data does not extend to the personal data within it. We therefore treat the producer names, addresses, and phone numbers in the registry under the Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562, on the lawful bases explained in our Privacy policy.

4. How to cite this site วิธีอ้างอิงเว็บไซต์นี้

If you use a number, finding, or visualisation from this site in academic, journalistic, or policy work, please cite both the source and this site. A complete citation looks like this:

one³ — OTOP registry intelligence, derived from the OTOP catalogue published by the Department of Community Development on data.go.th. Retrieved from https://otop.nonarkara.org/?view=crossrefs on 10 September 2026.

For a record-level citation, use the OTOP registry number and the permalink on the province dossier or record view.

These derived fields are not facts about the producer in the world; they are facts about the record as published. A "no contact channel" tier means the published record on data.go.th carries no phone number; it does not mean the producer has no phone. The distinction matters and we hold it.

6. Provenance per page การระบุแหล่งที่มาในทุกหน้า

Every record view and every province dossier carries a footer line of the form "Source: Department of Community Development, data.go.th · last refreshed on <date>". The site meta view (/?view=atlas → Record → "Last refreshed") shows the build timestamp. If a number on this site is wrong, the source is the place to correct it first; we will follow.
