2. What the site is เว็บไซต์นี้คืออะไร

The site is an intelligence surface over the public Thai OTOP (One Tambon One Product) registry. It reads the open CSV catalogue published by the Department of Community Development on data.go.th, normalises it, deduplicates it, and adds a small set of derived fields: a contact tier, a freshness stamp, and a province-level finding. It does not host a marketplace, does not process payments, and does not take orders.

The data on this site is not the same as a purchase recommendation. It is a registry read, intended to help a buyer, a researcher, a journalist, a development officer, or a curious citizen figure out who is in the registry, in which province, in which category, and whether the source record carries a phone number, an address, and a star grade.
