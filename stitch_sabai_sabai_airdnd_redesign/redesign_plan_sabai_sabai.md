# Redesign Plan: Sabai Sabai

## Current State Analysis
The current website ({{DATA:IMAGE:IMAGE_2}}) functions as a directory for massage and spa services in Thailand. It has a high-density, data-heavy layout with a dark theme. While functional, it lacks a premium, "spa-like" feel and the visual hierarchy could be significantly improved to make the discovery process more inviting.

## Vision for the Redesign: "Organic Minimalism"
Based on industry trends for 2026, I'm proposing a shift towards **Organic Minimalism**. This means moving away from the stark black background towards a palette of muted earth tones (sand, sage, charcoal) that evoke a sense of calm and wellness.

### Key Goals:
1. **Sensory Storytelling:** Use "scroll-telling" to sell the atmosphere of the spas, not just the technical specs.
2. **Improved Discovery:** Replace the dense grid with a more breathable, curated layout that prioritizes high-quality imagery.
3. **Premium Feel:** Utilize custom serif typography and subtle micro-animations to create a sense of luxury.
4. **Enhanced Navigation:** A split-screen homepage navigation that balances visual storytelling with powerful search/filtering.

## Proposed Screens
1. **Homepage / Landing:** A high-impact hero section that sets the tone, followed by featured collections (e.g., "Top Rated in Bangkok", "New & Noteworthy").
2. **Search & Directory:** A refined version of the current directory, with better filtering (by service type, location, price) and more elegant list/grid views.
3. **Spa Detail Page:** A dedicated view for each business featuring a high-res gallery, detailed service menu, verified reviews, and a clear call-to-action (e.g., "Book Now" or "Contact on Telegram").
4. **Interactive Map View:** A full-screen, high-fidelity map for discovering spas geographically, with integrated preview cards.
5. **Wellness Blog/Guide:** A curated section for local wellness tips, "secret spots," and trends, positioning the site as a local expert guide.
